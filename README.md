# Space Trader: Dealer's Hand

A 2D roguelike space trading and deck-building card game built with **Godot Engine 4.7** (GDScript).

## About

Trade goods across planets, build a combat deck, upgrade your ship, recruit crew, craft components, and complete quests. Win by meeting the **credit goal** (8000/10000/12000 based on difficulty), visiting all **7 planets**, installing a **T2 crafted upgrade**, and having **0 Bounty** to defeat the Pirate Lord.

## Features

- **Dynamic Trading** — Buy low, sell high across 5 planet types (Tech, Industrial, Agricultural, Mining, Outlaw) with drifting prices, faction reputation, and local trade loyalty.
- **Deck-Building Combat** — Energy-based card battles with telegraphed enemy intent, special effects, and growing difficulty.
- **Boarding Mechanics** — Board weakened enemy ships in a push-your-luck minigame to secure high-tier loot, at the risk of crew injuries.
- **Ship Upgrades & Crafting** — Expand stats at shipyards, buy new ships with unique abilities, and use Tech planets to craft exclusive T2 upgrades.
- **Crew System** — Recruit up to 3 crew members with passive bonuses (attack, defense, trading, smuggling) that synergize with your ship.
- **Quest System & Debt** — Procedural delivery quests with deadlines, reputation-based rewards, and the ability to take loans.
- **Bounty & Contraband** — Smuggle spice and stolen tech past customs, but beware of raising your heat and bounty.
- **World Events & Rivals** — Blockades, shortages, Pirate Lord Crimson Jack, and the recurring rival Captain Vex dynamically affect the galaxy.
- **Planet Missions** — Participate in risk/reward mini-games on planets (e.g., Hacking, Mining, Starport Defense).
- **Hybrid Visuals** — A mix of pre-rendered Sci-Fi PNG illustrations and procedural overlays (3D ship hulls, shaders, starfields).
- **Save/Load & Achievements** — JSON-based save system and global cross-run achievements.

## Running

1. Install [Godot 4.7](https://godotengine.org/)
2. Clone this repository
3. Open the project in Godot (Import -> select this directory)
4. Press F5 to run
